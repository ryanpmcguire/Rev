# Comments

- Keep comments terse. Prefer a short phrase over a sentence, a sentence over a paragraph.
- Do not write long block comments that narrate how the whole system works or make definitive, sweeping claims about behavior. Describe the immediate code, not the architecture's grand design.
- Match the comment density and style of the surrounding code.
- Do not leave stale comments after a change — update or delete them. No references to removed code/mechanisms.
- No restating the obvious (`// increment i`). Comment the non-obvious *why*, not the *what*.
